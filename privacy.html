Cookies are small text files that a website places on your device to store information about your preferences or browsing session. InsureCalcPro uses cookies for two primary purposes: site analytics and third-party advertising.

3.1 Analytics Cookies

We may use analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) to understand aggregate traffic patterns and usage behavior on our Site. To serve relevant ads, Google uses a cookie known as the DART cookie, which enables Google to show ads to users based on their visits to this Site and other websites on the Internet.

The DART cookie is subject to Google’s Privacy Policy, which governs its use in Google’s advertising and content network. Key points about this cookie:

  • › Google uses the DART cookie to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to insurecalcpro.com and other websites.
  • › Visitors may opt out of the use of the DART cookie for interest-based advertising by visiting Google’s Ad Settings at google.com/settings/ads.

  • › You may also opt out of third-party vendor use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info/choices (Digital Advertising Alliance) or networkadvertising.org/choices (Network Advertising Initiative).
